# Glossary

**A2A:** Account-to-account payment.

**Adaptive attacker:** A planner whose next campaign proposal depends on prior bounded observations.

**Agentic commerce:** Commerce in which a software agent acts under delegated user intent and constraints.

**Assurance range:** An isolated environment for testing controls against governed adversarial scenarios.

**Availability time:** The first time a data item is permitted to influence a decision.

**Campaign:** A causally related set of attacker actions, entities, and payment events.

**Champion:** The currently approved model and policy bundle used as the comparison reference.

**Challenger:** A candidate model or policy evaluated without automatic promotion.

**Decision owner:** The party authorized to take the payment or account action.

**Decision time:** The time at which a control produced its output.

**Deep generator:** A generative model used for conditional leaf distributions or content, not as a replacement for lifecycle state.

**Event time:** The time at which an activity occurred in the simulated world.

**Feature availability contract:** Declaration of a feature’s owner, source, timing, freshness, missing behavior, and permitted use.

**GenAI capability delta:** A measurable change in attacker cost, speed, scale, diversity, personalization, adaptation, coordination, or semantic manipulation caused by generative AI.

**Hidden generator:** A separately implemented generator used only after defender freeze. A shifted branch in the development simulator is not an independent hidden generator.

**Hidden validity oracle:** A non-observable evaluator that checks economic, causal, lifecycle, plausibility, and safety constraints.

**Ingestion time:** The time at which the platform received an event.

**Integrity failure:** A deterministic violation of identity, mandate, consent, scope, binding, nonce, expiry, or receipt requirements.

**Matured label:** A fraud, scam, dispute, recovery, or investigation outcome available by a declared training cutoff.

**Operational budget:** A limit on false declines, challenges, reviews, delays, latency, or analyst capacity.

**Past-only:** A feature or decision uses only information available strictly before or at its declared decision boundary.

**Payment lifecycle:** Initiation, authentication, authorization, clearing, settlement, report, dispute, recovery, and final loss states.

**Rail adapter:** A module that implements the fields, states, actions, and ownership semantics for a payment rail.

**Scenario bundle:** A versioned, typed, deterministic configuration produced from an approved threat card.

**Threat card:** A reviewed record connecting evidence, GenAI capability, payment mechanism, observables, simulation mapping, and defensive actions.

**Value conservation:** The rule that balances, settled inflows, settled outflows, fees, reversals, and residuals reconcile exactly.
